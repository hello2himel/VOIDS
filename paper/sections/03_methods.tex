\section{Methods}
Void centres recomputed from RA, Dec, and comoving distance $D$ (catalogue $h^{-1}$ lengths converted with $h=0.6766$, Planck18 $H_0=67.66$) in one consistent frame; stored-XYZ residual median $0.00$~Mpc (p90 $0.01$~Mpc). $R_{\rm v}\equiv R_{\max}$ core spheres (median $11.9\,h^{-1}{\rm Mpc}=17.6\,{\rm Mpc}$); $R_{\rm eff}$ as systematics branch. Membership via ball query, $r/R_{\max}=\min$, shell $0.8$--$1.0$ excluded from primary. Mask from \texttt{NSA\_main\_mask.pickle} (360,180 bool, 1-deg quantized). Edge test: eroding one cell removes 5.6\% of mask cells, 108/2367 SNe (3 void), 12/1163 void centres; cosmo void fraction shifts $0.0600\rightarrow0.0615$ (2.5\% relative, subdominant to Poisson 9\%). Randoms: 200000 uniform comoving ($z=0.01$--$0.114$) per seed (42,43,44), $\sim35200$ in-mask each; cos-weighted sky fraction $0.177$ (naive cell mean $0.141$ is wrong for equirectangular grids); expected $f_{\rm void}=0.1896\pm0.0018$ (seed spread), $f_{\rm shell}=0.166$, $f_{\rm wall}=0.627$ finite ($f_{\rm out}=0.018$); $V_{\rm eff,tot}=8.80\times10^{7}\,{\rm Mpc^{3}}$. Observed counts read from the parquet (no hand copies). Blinded SALT2 \citep{guy07} hierarchical $\Delta_{\rm HR}$ with Tripp standardisation \citep{tripp98}, peculiar-velocity covariance, mass-step joint fit and BTS efficiency planned; this version reports mask-corrected counts with randoms volumes and split apparent rate ratios only. Estimator: $R_{\rm env}=(N_{\rm env}/V_{\rm eff}f_{\rm env})/(N_{\rm wall}/V_{\rm eff}f_{\rm wall})$ with Poisson+Monte Carlo errors; sensitivity ${\rm SE}=\sigma\sqrt{1/N_{\rm v}+1/N_{\rm w}}$ ($\sigma=0.15$, $N_{\rm v}=118$ gives $0.028$ stat, $\sim0.035$ with floor; matched $N=27$ gives $\sim0.07$).
