\section{Introduction}
Cosmic voids fill most of the volume. Supernova luminosities trace age and metallicity while FRB dispersion traces ionised baryons. Acronyms: VAST (Void Analysis Toolkit), BTS (ZTF Bright Transient Survey), SALT2 (spectral-adaptive light-curve template), Tripp (luminosity standardisation relation), BBC (bias-correction simulations), PV (peculiar velocities), TNS (Transient Name Server), NSA (NASA-Sloan Atlas). Table~\ref{tab:prior} summarises prior work: void-transient demographics \citep{wang26,tsaprazi21}, ZTF Ia environments \citep{aubert25}, CHIME--large-scale-structure cross-correlations \citep{chime21}, and FRB void-baryon stacking \citep{sharma26}. No published paper jointly uses VoidFinder DR7 spheres \citep{douglass23} with flux-limited BTS \citep{perley20,fremling19} and CHIME in the same low-redshift volumes; closest is \citet{aubert25} (SDSS DR7 Voronoi densities with ZTF DR2 stretch); this work adds BTS flux-limited counts with blinded DR2 pilot differences in VAST spheres. Distances assume \citet{planck20} Planck18 ($H_0=67.66\,{\rm km\,s^{-1}\,Mpc^{-1}}$, $\Omega_m=0.3097$); void-catalog lengths in $h^{-1}$ units use $h=0.6766$ throughout. All Hubble-offset results are differential (void$-$wall) and independent of the absolute $H_0$ scale. Voids are VAST VoidFinder maximal spheres with $R>10\,h^{-1}$Mpc.
