We cross-match VAST VoidFinder SDSS DR7 (1163 voids, $z<0.114$, median $R_{\max}=11.9\,h^{-1}{\rm Mpc}=17.6\,{\rm Mpc}$, median $R_{\rm eff}=15.5\,h^{-1}{\rm Mpc}=22.9\,{\rm Mpc}$) with ZTF Bright Transient Survey supernovae using a Planck18-consistent ($h=0.6766$) KD-tree ball query with 1-deg SDSS mask (quantized; edge test in text). Of 6873 BTS transients at $z=0.01$--$0.114$, 2465 lie in-mask: 147 void ($r/R_{\max}<0.8$), 195 shell ($0.8$--$1.0$), 2080 wall (43 outside). Cosmo SNe (TDE/Ca-rich/other excluded): 2367 total, 142 void (118 SN Ia; subtype audit pending TNS). Volume-limited $z\le0.06$: 1270 SNe total, 69 void (46 Ia). Raw Poisson sensitivity to a void--wall Hubble offset is $\sim0.035$ mag at 95 per cent including systematics floor; blinded SALT2 standardisation with mass, peculiar-velocity and selection controls is in progress. CHIME Catalog 1 (600 bursts, 524 sources) is used for footprint and error-budget demonstration only; unlocalised $z<0.114$ stacking is underpowered by $>10\times$ and joint constraints are deferred to Catalog 2. Catalog, randoms design and pipeline will be released.
