\section{Discussion and conclusions}
The masked BTS sample yields 2367 cosmology SNe (142 void, 187 shell, 1996 wall) against a randoms expectation $f_{\rm void}=0.190$: an apparent void/wall ratio $0.24$ overall ($0.26$ Ia, $0.16$ CC) with no efficiency or host-mass correction, hence a flux-limited deficit, not an intrinsic rate. The in-mask transient fraction $2465/6873=0.359$ exceeds the geometric sky fraction $0.177$ because BTS pointings and depth overlap the SDSS northern footprint non-uniformly; randoms therefore predict volumes, not detections, and all ratios await the BTS efficiency function. The void redshift offset (median $0.064$ vs $0.056$) is the Malmquist gradient in the same data. Limitations: 1-deg mask quantization, pending TNS subtype purge and spec-$z$ audit, pending peculiar-velocity and host-mass corrections, pending null/injection validation, and an FRB arm that is a forecast only (Catalog 2 required). The blinded SALT2 $\Delta_{\rm HR}$ analysis, with Tripp standardisation and systematics budget, will decide whether voids bias SN distances; until unblinding, no Hubble-offset conclusion is drawn.
