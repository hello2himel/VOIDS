\section{Discussion and conclusions}
The masked BTS sample yields 2367 cosmology SNe (142 void, 187 shell, 1996 wall) against a randoms expectation $f_{\rm void}=0.190$: an apparent void/wall ratio $0.24$ overall ($0.26$ Ia, $0.16$ CC) with no efficiency or host-mass correction, hence a flux-limited deficit, not an intrinsic rate. The in-mask transient fraction $2465/6873=0.359$ exceeds the geometric sky fraction $0.177$ because BTS pointings and depth overlap the SDSS northern footprint non-uniformly; randoms therefore predict volumes, not detections, and all ratios await the BTS efficiency function. The void redshift offset (median $0.064$ vs $0.056$) is the Malmquist gradient in the same data. First blinded DR2 test ($N=396$, 31 void, finite wall): raw $\Delta_{\rm HR}=+0.057\pm0.026$ falls to $+0.023\pm0.034$ mass+$z$ matched (27 voids); joint fit recovers the mass slope ($-0.065\pm0.011$) with residual void offset $+0.037\pm0.036$ — a null with an understood confound, the strongest position for this sample size. Limitations: 1-deg mask quantization, pending TNS subtype purge and spec-$z$ audit, pending peculiar-velocity flow-model and host-mass-step refinements, and an FRB arm that is a forecast only (Catalog 2 required). Unblinding protocol: thresholds are passing RA-shuffle/block-bootstrap nulls (done: $p=0.48$, bootstrap validates error), a closed systematics budget, and frozen cuts; the blind key rotates at unblinding; until then no Hubble-offset conclusion is drawn.
