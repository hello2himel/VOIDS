\section{Discussion and conclusions}
The masked BTS sample yields \NCosmo\ cosmology SNe (\NVoidCosmo\ void, \NShellCosmo\ shell, \NWallCosmo\ wall) against a randoms expectation $f_{\rm void}=0.190$: an apparent void/wall ratio $0.24$ overall ($0.26$ Ia, $0.16$ CC) with no efficiency or host-mass correction, hence a flux-limited deficit, not an intrinsic rate. The in-mask transient fraction $2465/6873=0.359$ exceeds the geometric sky fraction $0.177$ because BTS pointings and depth overlap the SDSS northern footprint non-uniformly; randoms therefore predict volumes, not detections, and all ratios await the BTS efficiency function. The void redshift offset (median $0.064$ vs $0.056$) is the Malmquist gradient in the same data. Primary Hubble test is full-volume DR2-$z$ (752 in-mask, 37 void): matched $\Delta_{\rm HR}=+0.034\pm0.030$ mag (stat, $n=33$), $\pm0.042$ with systematics, consistent with zero (two-sided 95\% interval $[-0.05,+0.12]$ mag). The BTS-gated branch agrees ($+0.023\pm0.034$, $n=27$); joint fit recovers the mass slope ($-0.065\pm0.011$) — a null with an understood confound. Limitations: 1-deg mask quantization, TNS-key join unavailable (HR arm purged via BTS+DR2 strings, 0/396 peculiar; counts arm carries BTS-string audit only), PV flow-model bounded analytically, and an FRB arm that is a forecast only (Catalog 2 required). Unblinding protocol: thresholds are passing RA-shuffle/block-bootstrap nulls (done: $p=0.48$, bootstrap validates error), a closed systematics budget, and frozen cuts; the blind key rotates at unblinding; unblinded 2026-10-01: matched offset $+0.034\pm0.030$ mag (stat), $\pm0.042$ with systematics, consistent with zero; two-sided 95\% interval $[-0.05,+0.12]$ mag at this sample size.

\subsection*{FRB stacking outlook}
FRB error budget (Cat1, following \citet{macquart20} and void-decrement simulations \citep{walker24}): per-sightline $\sigma_{\rm tot}\sim150$--$200\,{\rm pc\,cm^{-3}}$ (IGM 80--120, host 80--150, Milky Way residual 20--40 with NE2001$-$YMW16 median $-3.3$, std $37.5$, p90 $68.1$, halo 20). Void signal $\sim10$--$30$. A $\delta{\rm DM}<50$ limit at 95 per cent needs $N_{\rm void}\gtrsim36$ ($\sim200$ localised total); unlocalised $z<0.114$ needs $\sim900$ void sightlines, have $\sim30$--$50$.
